# Sample documents

The two sample drawings in this folder are extracted from "Virginia's Farmhouse",
a freely published drawing set by Open Source Blueprints (original designer
Jay Osborne, 2014), hosted on Wikimedia Commons as
`File:Farmhouse Drawing Set V-001.pdf` and licensed CC BY-SA 3.0:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Farmhouse_Drawing_Set_V-001.pdf

- `plan-levels.pdf`: foundation, first floor, second floor and roof plans (sheets A1.1 to A1.4)
- `plan-elevations.pdf`: elevations and sections (sheets A2.0 to A3.2)

They are stand-in drawings used only to demonstrate the viewer. Replace them
with your own plans for a real evaluation.
